Add `@caucejs/hosting`: a builder with the environment, the configuration and the container; hosted services that start in the order listed and stop in reverse within a timeout; a container validated in every environment; and a `Lifetime` contract. `@caucejs/node` adds `ProcessLifetime`, on `SIGINT` and `SIGTERM`, and `standardSources`. `Config.add` takes several sources at once.
